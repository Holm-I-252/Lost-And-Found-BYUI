Someone may have already reported your item. Browse the board before you post.

Name the building

Write the building and spot, like “STC, 2nd floor” or “I-Center lobby”. That’s how your report lands on the campus map.

📸

Add a photo link

A picture helps a lot. Share it from Google Drive or Photos and make sure anyone with the link can see it.

🛡️

Keep one detail back

Everything you write is public. Never post card or ID numbers, and keep one detail to yourself to check the real owner.
