About FoundIt

Five BYU-Idaho students, one simple idea: things lost on campus should find their way home.

Our mission: make it simple to help people find their lost items.

Things get lost on every campus: keys in the library, a water bottle in a classroom, a backpack in the food court. Usually someone finds them, but the finder and the owner never connect.

FoundIt gives both sides one place to look. Reporting takes a minute, every report is searchable, and the campus map shows where things turn up.

How we built it

Agile, one sprint at a time

We planned in Scrum sprints, tracked every task on a shared board, and shipped small improvements often.

🗂️

Sprint planning

User stories and tasks lived on our Lucid team board, so everyone knew what was next.

📝

Google Forms & Sheets

Reports come in through Google Forms and go straight into a sheet that powers the board.

🌐

GitHub Pages

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, reviewed through pull requests and published on GitHub Pages.

🗺️

Campus map

Building locations come from OpenStreetMap, so each report can land on the right spot.
