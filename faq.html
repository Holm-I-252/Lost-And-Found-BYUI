Questions? We’ve got you.

Everything about reporting, finding and claiming items. Type below to jump straight to an answer.

What is FoundIt?

FoundIt is a lost-and-found board for the BYU-Idaho campus. People who lose something post it, people who find something post it, and anyone can search the board to bring the two together.

Is this the official BYU-Idaho lost and found?

No. FoundIt is a student class project, not an official BYU-Idaho service. If you lost something valuable, also ask at the front desk of the building where you lost it.

How do I report something I lost?

Go to Report → I lost something and fill out the short form. Say what it is, the building where you last had it, and the date. A photo link helps a lot.

I found something. What should I do?

Thank you! Open Report → I found something, describe the item, where you found it and where it is now. Keep one small detail to yourself so you can check that the person who claims it is the real owner.

How do I prove an item is mine?

Describe something that isn’t in the public report: your lock screen, what’s inside the bag, a scratch, a sticker or a name. The full steps are on How to claim.

Who can see my report?

Anyone who visits FoundIt can see the item details: what it is, the place, the date and the photo. Never put card numbers, ID numbers, passwords or other private information in the description.

Why isn’t my item on the campus map?

The map places a report when its location names a campus building we know, like “STC”, “Manwaring Center” or “I-Center”. Reports with other places still show up as cards on Browse.

How fast does my report show up?

Reports come from a Google Sheet that updates every few minutes. Give it a moment, then refresh the Browse page.

What are the “Sample” items?

Examples that show how the board looks when it’s busy. They always carry a “Sample” badge, and you can switch them off on the Browse page.

I got my item back! Now what?

Awesome! Confirm it here so we can mark the report as returned. Returned items show up with their own badge, which helps everyone trust the board.

Who made FoundIt?

A team of five BYU-Idaho students built it as a class project, working in Scrum sprints. Meet us on the About page.
