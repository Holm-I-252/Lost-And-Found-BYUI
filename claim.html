How to claim an item

Five simple steps keep it quick for you and safe for the finder. The line fills in as you scroll.

  1. 1

    🔎 Search the board

    Open Browse and filter by what you lost, where, and when. Try the campus map if you remember the building but not the exact spot.

    See found items
  2. 2

    🧐 Check the details

    Open the report and compare the color, brand, stickers and place. Check where the item is now: still with the finder, or left at a front desk.

  3. 3

    🔐 Prove it’s yours

    Be ready to describe something that isn’t in the report: your phone’s lock screen, what’s inside the wallet, a scratch or a name inside the cover. Finders are asked to keep one detail back for exactly this.

  4. 4

    🏫 Meet somewhere public

    Pick it up in a busy campus spot during the day, like a building lobby, or at the front desk where it was turned in. Bring a friend if you like.

  5. 5

    🎉 Close the report

    Got it back? Let the FoundIt team know so we can mark the report as returned. A returned item tells everyone the board works.

Stay safe

A few rules we always follow

Most people are just trying to help. These habits keep it that way.

Never pay to get it back

FoundIt is free. If someone asks for money or a “reward” first, stop and talk to the team.

Keep private info private

Don’t share passwords, card numbers or your home address. A name and a campus meeting spot are enough.

Public places only

Meet on campus, in daylight, where other people are around. Never go to someone’s apartment for a pickup.

Check before you hand over

Finders: ask for the hidden detail first. If the answer doesn’t match, keep the item and tell the team.
