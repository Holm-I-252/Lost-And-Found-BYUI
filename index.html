How it works

Three steps to get it back

No account and no app to install. Just a short form and a board everyone on campus can search.

01
📝

Report it

Lost something or found something? Fill out a short form: what it is, where, and when.

02
🔎

Search the board

Every report shows up on the board. Filter by what, where and when, or look at the campus map.

03
🤝

Get it home

Prove it’s yours with a detail only the owner would know, then meet somewhere safe on campus.
